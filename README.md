# Ground-Source Heat Pump Building Services Design

**Michael Ogochukwu Ugwu · BSc Mechanical Engineering · MATE, Hungary · 2026**

> **The mechanical drawings and detailed design documentation are in the annexes at the end of the thesis PDF.**

This thesis develops the building-services design for a **133.45 m² family house in Tordas, Hungary**, using a ground-source heat pump.

The work covers:

- Heating and cooling, including underfloor heating, ceiling cooling and supplementary split air-conditioning.
- Domestic hot water, water supply and drainage.
- Equipment selection, pipework layouts and the utility-room hydraulic schematic.
- Thermal analysis, manual calculation checks and installation-cost assessment.

**Tools:** AutoCAD and WinWatt.

**Modelled results:** For annual heating and domestic hot water, the proposed system showed approximately **67% lower purchased energy** and **46.9% lower associated CO₂ emissions** than a condensing gas boiler, under the study assumptions.
